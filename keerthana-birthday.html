Your Story

A Little Something I’ve Kept in My Heart ❤️

Some feelings are difficult to put into words…

I don’t even know where to begin, because there are so many things I’ve wanted to tell you and so many feelings I’ve kept inside for so long.

Nine years ago, I fell for you at first sight. I don’t know if you remember that time, or if you ever realized what you would come to mean to me. But I remember. And somehow, after all these years, I still find myself feeling the same way.

I’ve loved you in so many ways—as a person, as a sportsperson, as a friend, as a classmate, and simply as you. Not just because of how I felt when I first saw you, but because of the person you are. Every side of you has given me another reason to love you.

I wanted to talk to you more, spend more time with you, and create more memories with you. And that’s why I started pulling pranks on you. But now, I want to be honest with you: all those pranks I pulled on you weren’t really just pranks. They were my way of getting closer to you and trying to understand how you felt about me, because I never had the courage to ask you directly.

I still remember every single moment with you. I still smile when I think about them while looking at your picture every night before sleeping. Sometimes, I regret not telling you all this sooner. And more than anything, I want to thank you for all the memories you’ve given me, even the ones you might not remember anymore.

I don’t know if you’ll ever understand how much I’ve kept inside these nine years. There are feelings I could fill an entire galaxy with, and somehow, even all those stars wouldn’t be enough to explain them.

I know you may already have someone special in your life. And even if you don’t, you may see me only as a friend, or as a brother. I understand that, and I accept it.

I’m not telling you this to change how you feel, or to ask anything of you. I just didn’t want to carry it in silence any longer, and I wanted you to hear it from me.

I fell for you nine years ago, Keerthana. Nine years later, I still love you, and I will love you.

Forever and ever and ever. ❤️
